untype · user guide
01
What untype is
A tool for one person on one Mac: speak instead of typing, in any app, in Greek and in English.
Section 01
01 · Why
Typing is the slowest part of the day.

A message, a prompt or a note is spoken in seconds and typed in minutes. The dictation built into the Mac leaves raw text behind, and a meeting leaves nothing behind at all.

  • Spoken text has filler words, restarts and half sentences that have to be cleaned by hand.
  • Daily work mixes Greek and English, and the reader often needs the other language.
  • The text has to land where the cursor is, in the app you are working in, not in a separate window.
  • What was said in a call is gone unless somebody kept notes.
Section 01
01 · What it does
Three things untype does for you.
01

It types what you say

Hold a hotkey and speak, or tap it and speak hands-free. The words appear at the cursor while you talk, in whatever app is in front.

02

It works on the text

A language model cleans up what you said, translates it between Greek and English, or answers it. The same works on text you have selected, without speaking.

03

It records your meetings

Both sides of a call become a transcript you can read as it is made, with the audio, your attachments and an assistant that answers questions about the meeting.

untype lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon. Everything is controlled from three places: the hotkey, the small overlay at the bottom of the screen, and the dropdown under the menu-bar icon.
Section 01
01 · Where it works
One Mac, every app, two languages.
Where it types
  • Mail, Notes, Outlook, Teams, documents and browser text fields.
  • Chat apps and coding agents in the browser or in a terminal.
  • Any text field of macOS 14 or later.
Greek and English
  • You can speak either language, also in the same sentence.
  • Translate turns the text into the other language, or always into English, or always into Greek.
  • Meeting transcripts tell the two languages apart per line.
What it needs
  • A key of a speech-recognition provider: Soniox, ElevenLabs or Google.
  • For Refine, Translate and Ask, a key of a language-model provider.
  • A few macOS permissions, granted once.
Where your words go
  • The sound goes to the speech provider you chose; the text goes to a language model only when an operator asks for it.
  • Keys, prompts, your dictionary and the meeting files stay on your Mac.
  • untype's own logs never hold what you said.

Section 02
02 · The hotkey
One hotkey, two ways to dictate.
You doWhat happens
Hold the hotkey, speak, releaseThe text is typed into the field the cursor is in. This is push-to-talk.
Tap the hotkey (shorter than 0.4 s)Recording goes on with your hands free. The overlay shows how to finish.
… then EnterThe text is typed and Return is pressed in that field: a chat sends it, a terminal submits it, a document gets a new line.
… then the hotkey againThe text is typed, nothing is sent.
… then EscNothing is delivered and the words typed so far are removed.
The hotkey is Control+` unless you change it in Settings › Push-to-Talk, and it is off until you switch it on. There is no mode to choose: how long you press decides.
Section 02
02 · Where the text goes
The words appear while you speak.
1

Typed live

What you say is typed at the cursor as it is recognised, so you see at once whether you were heard right.

2

Replaced when you finish

If Refine, Translate or Ask is on, the live words are replaced in place by the result. A small moving line under the cursor shows that the model is working.

3

Delivered where you started

The text goes to the app that was in front when you pressed the hotkey. You may switch to another app meanwhile: untype puts the text where it belongs and brings you back.

4

Or to the clipboard

With the Clipboard operator on, the result is also copied, ready to paste anywhere.

Section 02
02 · Dictating into existing text
Dictation that respects what is already there.
In the middle of a sentence

The whole sentence is refined

  • Put the cursor inside a sentence and dictate with Refine on.
  • untype refines the words before the cursor, what you said and the words after it, and replaces the sentence with the result.
  • Where the app does not allow it, only your words are inserted.
Correct as you go

Your edits are kept

  • While dictating you can move the cursor, type over a word or erase one.
  • When the turn ends, the model gets the text as it stands in the field, so corrections survive and words stay where you put them.
Hands-free punctuation

You end the sentences

  • Settings › Push-to-Talk › I end the sentences: no full stop at every pause, a capital only after a full stop.
  • Full stop after a long pause closes the sentence after about four seconds of silence.
These work in fields that let untype read their text: native macOS fields and most browser fields. Terminals and some apps do not; there the dictated words are simply typed.

  • Drag the tray to move the overlay; double-click it to send it back to the bottom centre.

Six switches decide what happens to your words.

RefineRCleans up what you said: grammar, filler words, restarts. Same meaning, same language.Until changed
TranslateTTurns the text into the other language, or always into English or Greek.Until changed
AskAWhat you say is a request to the model; its answer is typed in place of your words.This turn

Switch an operator by clicking its chip on the overlay, with the hotkey plus its letter while dictating, in the menu-bar dropdown or in Settings › Operators. This turn means it switches off again after one dictation; Until changed keeps it as you set it. You choose which one per operator.
Section 02
02 · Refine, Translate, Ask
Talk to the model, not just through it.
R · T

Clean text, in the language you need

  • Refine never answers what you said and never changes the language.
  • Translate follows the direction you set: the other language, to English or to Greek.
  • With both on, you get the cleaned and translated text in one step.
A · Ask

Dictate a request, get the answer

  • "Write a short reply thanking them and proposing Tuesday." The reply is typed where the cursor is.
  • With Translate on, the answer is written in the target language.
  • Ask switches itself off after one turn, so the next dictation is plain text again.
L · Alternative LLM

A second model, one key away

  • Set a second provider and model in Settings › Language Models › Alternative.
  • With L on, the turn uses it: a fast model for everyday text, a stronger one when it matters.
  • If no alternative is set, the text is delivered as spoken and untype tells you.
If the model cannot be reached or is not set up, you never lose your words: the dictated text is delivered as it was heard, with a warning.
Section 02
02 · Experimental operators
Give the model more to work with.
F · Follow-up

Continue the conversation

Ask also sends your earlier requests and answers: "shorter", "more formal", "add a closing line". New conversation in the dropdown starts over.

S · Selection

Include the selected text

The text you selected goes along, either as the material to rework and replace, or only as context for what you dictate.

W · Screenshot

Show the window

A picture of the window you are in goes with the request, so you can ask about what you see. It is also copied to the clipboard.

X · Window text

Send the whole text

The text of the window, including what is scrolled out of view, goes along: "summarise this email", with a long email open.

Off until you ask for them. Switch them on together in Settings › Operators › Experimental operators (also in the dropdown). Each one then appears as a chip and stays off until you switch it on. Screenshot needs the Screen & System Audio Recording permission.
Section 02
02 · Push-to-Act
Work on text you already have, without speaking.
1

Select text and press the second hotkey

Control+Option+` unless you change it in Settings › Push-to-Act. Nothing is recorded.

2

Choose in the pop-up

The model (L switches between primary and alternatives), the operators (R T A F W C) and the translation direction (1 the other language, 2 English, 3 Greek).

3

Enter runs, Esc cancels

The result replaces the selection. Your choices are remembered for next time, separately from the dictation operators.

4

Nothing selected?

The whole text of the field is used. In a terminal, the prompt you have typed to a coding agent is used.

Section 02
02 · Being heard right
Your words, and only your voice.

Dictionary (Settings › Speech)

  • Add the names, abbreviations and terms that are not heard right, written as they must be written.
  • Teach: say only that word for a few seconds. If it is still heard differently, untype remembers that form and writes your word in its place from then on.

Only my voice (Settings › Speech › Other voices)

  • Press Learn in a quiet moment and read a sentence aloud. untype keeps a few seconds of your voice on your Mac.
  • From then on, when other people talk around you, only your words are written. Works with Soniox.

Meetings

Section 03
03 · Recording
Record both sides of a call.
1

Start

Hover the overlay and click the record button, or use the menu-bar dropdown › Meeting › Record meeting. It works with Teams, Zoom or anything else that plays sound.

2

What is captured

Your microphone, labelled You, and the sound of the other apps, with the speakers told apart as Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on. Greek and English are recognised per sentence.

3

While it runs

The overlay stays on screen with a red trace and the elapsed time. Dictation and Push-to-Act keep working, and what you dictate stays out of the meeting.

4

Stop

Click stop, or let it end by itself after three minutes without speech.

5

Name it

A card shows where it was saved, with Open and Name. Say in a few words what the meeting was about and untype gives it a short title.

Section 03
03 · The transcription window
Read the meeting as it is being said.
Live transcript

Open it with ⌘3

  • From the dropdown, or the captions icon in the overlay's tray.
  • Both, Microphone or System chooses the side.
  • Follow keeps the newest text in view; switch it off to read back while text is still added.
  • Copy takes what was said so far; Clear empties the window, never the files.
Pause a side

Keep something off the record

  • Pause on the Microphone tab stops your side; on the System tab, the call's side.
  • While paused, nothing of that side is transcribed or saved. The other side goes on.
  • Resume picks it up again; the times stay aligned.
Attach

Put material into the meeting

  • Attach saves what is on the clipboard at that moment: a picture, text or copied files.
  • Drop files on the transcript, or use Attach a file.
  • Each attachment appears at its place in the conversation with a thumbnail, and opens on a click.
A screenshot of the slide being discussed, the document under review, a number from a spreadsheet: attach them while they are talked about, and they stay tied to that minute of the meeting.
Section 03
03 · The meeting assistant
Ask the meeting a question.

Open Meeting assistant with ⌘4, or click Ask in the transcription window, and ask about the meeting being recorded or the last one.

  • "What did we agree about the deadline?" "List the open points with their owners." "What did I miss in the last ten minutes?"
  • It reads the transcript as it grows and your attachments: pictures, text, PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
  • It only reads. It never changes the transcript or the files.
  • One conversation per meeting, kept when untype restarts and when the meeting is renamed.
  • It answers with its own model, chosen in Settings › Meetings › Assistant. Nothing else to install.

Transcript

Audio

Both sides, one side or none; in separate files or mixed in one. About 15 MB per hour and file, aligned with the transcript's times.

Apple Notes

Optionally the transcript is also written as a note in the folder "untype" of Apple Notes when the meeting ends.

Text and audio reach the files while the meeting runs, so they can be opened during the meeting and survive a crash. Recording other people can need their consent: ask first.
Section 03
03 · Outlook calendar
untype knows which meeting you are in.
Reminder

A card when a Teams meeting starts

  • "Starting now · Teams · 6 people", with the subject, Record and Dismiss.
  • It stays for the whole meeting until you choose, steps aside while you dictate, and never shows while you are already recording.
  • Only meetings you organise or accepted count; declined and unanswered invitations do not.
Name

The recording is named after the meeting

  • A recording that starts during a meeting, or up to ten minutes before it, gets a title made from its subject and participants.
  • The transcript begins with the meeting's subject, time, organiser and participants.
  • Rename on the saved card still replaces the name.
Switch it on in Settings › Meetings › Calendar › Use my Outlook calendar. It reads the calendar through the nbg-m365 command-line tool, which keeps its own Microsoft 365 sign-in; without that tool the switch stays off and Settings says so.
Section 03
03 · A clean transcript
Who said what, even without headphones.

Through loudspeakers the microphone also hears the call, so other people's words can appear a second time as yours. Headphones avoid it. Three switches in Settings › Meetings help when you have none:

  • Remove speaker sound from the microphone takes what the Mac plays out of your side. The app you are calling with hears you as usual.
  • Only my voice in the microphone's transcript keeps your side to your own words, using the voice sample learned in Settings › Speech.
  • Mute the microphone while dictating (on by default) keeps what you dictate out of the meeting's transcript and audio.

Install and set up
Ten minutes, once: install the app, give it its keys, and let macOS know what it may do.
Section 04
04 · Install
Two ways in. Both give you the same app.
Homebrew

Two commands in Terminal

brew tap biks2013-tools/untype

brew install --cask untype

Later, brew upgrade --cask untype brings the newest build.

Disk image

Download and drag

Get untype-0.1.0.dmg from github.com/biks2013-tools/untype-s/releases/latest, open it and drag untype onto Applications. To upgrade, replace the app with the newer one.

The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a warning, on macOS 14 or later. Your settings, keys and permissions are kept across upgrades. Which build is running is written at the bottom of the dropdown and of the Settings sidebar.

To hear you. macOS asks at the first recording.

Section 04 · screenshot of build 87
04 · Permissions, step by step
Where to click in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
1 · Microphone
  • Hold the hotkey once. macOS asks whether untype may use the microphone: click Allow.
  • No question, or refused earlier? Open Microphone and switch untype on.
2 · Accessibility
  • Open Accessibility (macOS 27: Device Control and Data Access).
  • Click +, choose Applications › untype, and make sure its switch is on.
  • Quit untype and open it again.
3 · Input Monitoring
  • Needed only when the hotkey stays silent in front of other apps.
  • Open Input Monitoring, click +, add untype, switch it on.
  • Quit untype and open it again.
4 · Screen & System Audio
  • untype asks at the first meeting recording.
  • Open Screen & System Audio Recording and switch untype on.
  • Start the recording again.
You do not have to find these pages yourself: Settings › Permissions & Keys › Open Permission Setup… shows this guide with a button that opens each page, and re-checks the state for you.
Section 04
04 · When a permission stops working
Switched on, but macOS still says no.

What you see

The hotkey stops reacting, or the words are heard but not typed, although untype is switched on in System Settings. It can happen after installing a different copy of the app.

What to do

  • In the page of that permission, select untype and click − to remove it.
  • Click + and add untype from Applications again.
  • Quit untype and open it again.

Repair only the permission that is broken. Removing the others means granting them again.

Section 04
04 · Provider keys
Setting the keys.
In the app

Settings › Permissions & Keys › Edit Keys…

  • Also behind Set keys… on the welcome window.
  • Type or paste each key. Keys are hidden as you type; a Show switch reveals them.
  • Save writes only what you changed. Emptying a field removes that key.
  • The pane shows whether a key is set and where it comes from, never the key itself.
In the file

For those who prefer a text editor

  • The keys live in ~/.tool-agents/untype/.env, a file only you can read.
  • untype creates it with every setting listed and explained, each line starting with #. Remove the # and fill in the value.
  • Optional expiry dates such as SONIOX_API_KEY_EXPIRES_AT make untype warn you two weeks before a key runs out.
untype never guesses. If a key that a feature needs is missing, it says which one, instead of quietly using something else. Keys never appear in logs.

Section 05
05 · Push-to-Talk
The hotkey, and how a dictation ends.
  • Enable push-to-talk switches the hotkey on or off, like the switch in the dropdown.
  • Hotkey: click the field and press the combination you want, for example Control+` or a single key such as F16.
  • Hands-free (after a tap): I end the sentences and Full stop after a long pause decide who punctuates.
  • Quick Close: on release, deliver what was heard so far without waiting for the last correction. Faster; the last words can be a little less accurate.
  • Test shows whether the hotkey listener is ready, and lets you try a dictation without the hotkey.

Staying current

Changing the provider sets its current model. Latest does the same later, and a model the provider has retired is replaced by its successor.

Voice commands

On: spoken phrases such as "command send" or "command translate on" are obeyed. Switch them off if you dictate text that contains those words; then everything you say is text.

Section 05
05 · Operators
How each operator starts, and how long it lasts.
  • Processing: Refine, Translate, Ask and Alternative LLM, each with its letter and a switch.
  • Delivery: Clipboard and Focused input.
  • Lasts, per operator: Until changed keeps it as you set it; This turn switches it off again after one dictation. Ask starts as This turn.
  • Translation › Direction: the other language, to English, or to Greek.
  • Experimental operators: one switch for Follow-up, Selection, Screenshot and Window text. While it is on, Selected text is chooses whether a selection is the material to rework or only context.
Section 05 · screenshot of build 87
05 · Language Models
A main model and two alternatives.
  • Primary: Use a language model, then the provider and the model. It serves Refine, Translate and Ask, and writes the English transcript and the titles of meetings.
  • Alternative: the model the L operator switches to.
  • Alternative 2: a third choice offered in the Push-to-Act pop-up.
  • Providers: azure-openai, google (Gemini), anthropic and azure-anthropic (Claude, direct or on Microsoft Foundry), deepseek.
  • Model: the provider's name for it, for example gemini-3.8-flash or deepseek-flash; on Azure, the name of your deployment.
  • An alternative needs both a provider and a model, and that provider's key.

Section 05
05 · Prompts
Change how the model treats your words.
  • The pane lists the instructions the model gets, by purpose: Refinement, Translation, Refine + translate, Ask, Selected text, Screenshot, Window text, Transcription context.
  • Edit one and press Save (⌘S). The next dictation already uses it; nothing has to be restarted.
  • Revert drops what you have not saved. Reset to Default brings back the original text.
  • A prompt that is missing a required placeholder such as {text} cannot be saved, so you cannot break dictation from here.
  • Typical changes: a preferred tone for Refine, terms that must never be translated, a house style for Ask answers.

Section 06
06 · The daily loop
Click into a field, speak, and move on.
01

Set the turn

Glance at the chips. Refine and Input usually stay on; click Translate, Ask or L when this turn needs them.

02

Hold, or tap

Hold the hotkey for a sentence or two. Tap it for a long message or when your hands are busy.

03

Watch and correct

The words appear as you speak. Change your mind mid-turn: press a letter to flip an operator, or fix a word with the keyboard.

04

Finish

Release, or after a tap press Enter to send, the hotkey to keep, Esc to drop. The result replaces the live words.

In a meeting, the same loop still works: start the recording from the overlay's tray, keep dictating into your notes or the chat, and ask the assistant when you need to catch up.

06 · When it does not work · setup
What you see, why, and what to do.
What you seeWhyWhat to do
The hotkey does nothingThe hotkey is switched off; it starts that way.Menu-bar icon › Hotkey on.
The hotkey works only while untype is in frontInput Monitoring is missing.Add untype under Input Monitoring; quit and reopen.
The words are heard but not typedAccessibility is missing or no longer valid.Grant it, or remove and add untype again; quit and reopen.
A message about a missing key at the startNo key for the chosen speech provider.Settings › Permissions & Keys › Edit Keys…
The text arrives exactly as spoken, with a warningThe language model is off, or its key is missing.Settings › Language Models; then the keys.
With L on, the text is not processedNo alternative model is set.Set a provider and a model under Alternative.
The meeting transcript has only YouScreen & System Audio Recording is missing.Grant it and start the recording again.
The calendar switch will not stay onThe nbg-m365 tool is not installed or not signed in.Install it and sign in; Settings shows the reason.
Section 06
06 · When it does not work · daily use
Small surprises, and the switch behind each one.
What you seeWhyWhat to do
Ask switched itself offAsk lasts one turn unless told otherwise.Operators › Lasts › Until changed.
The chips F, S, W, X are not thereThe experimental operators are off.Operators › Experimental operators.
An operator flips while you dictateYou said a command phrase such as "command translate on".Speech › Voice commands off.
A name is always written wrongSpeech recognition does not know it.Speech › Dictionary › add it, then Teach.
A colleague's words get typedThe microphone hears everyone.Speech › Only my voice › Learn.
The model takes long, then the raw text arrivesThe model did not answer in time.Nothing is lost; try again, or switch to L.
A long Ask answer is cut offIt exceeded the answer length limit.Raise UNTYPE_ASK_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS in the .env file.
Other people appear as You in a meetingThe microphone hears the loudspeakers.Headphones, or the clean-transcript switches.
A recording stopped by itselfThree minutes passed without speech.Meetings › Stop after silence.
